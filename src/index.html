(02) Why we exist

Most teams don't need another AI tool. They need the tools they already have to understand their orders, their parts and their people. We make AI native to the way your business already runs.

(03) Approach

Start with one workflow. Grow from there.

Four steps, each one earning the next. You can stop after any of them and keep everything we built.

Assess

  1. 012 weeks

    Assess

    We sit with your team, map where the hours go and rank the workflows worth automating by payback, not by novelty.

    • Workflow map
    • Data readiness check
    • Ranked opportunity list
  2. 024–6 weeks

    Prove

    We automate one workflow end to end on your real data, with your people checking every output before it counts.

    • Working pilot
    • Before and after time study
    • Go or no-go call
  3. 038–16 weeks

    Build

    We connect the systems behind it, including your ERP, inbox and documents, and roll it out to the teams that need it.

    • Connectors
    • Knowledge layer with permissions
    • Agents with human review
  4. 04Monthly

    Run

    We watch quality, fix what drifts and add the next workflow. You own the code, the data and the prompts.

    • Evals and monitoring
    • Monthly report
    • Next workflow
